About The Bogey Train

The Bogey Train is an independent golf architecture journal written by Sam Collett. The site examines golf courses as works of art, evaluating them on compositional merit: routing logic, strategic design, pacing, aesthetic integration, and the relationship between a course and the land it occupies.

Golf architecture criticism borrows from the same evaluative frameworks used in film, music, and literature. A course's middle stretch can be assessed the way a film's second act is; a routing can be studied the way an album's sequencing is. The best designs reveal their identity through subtlety, and this site attempts to uncover those qualities through close analysis.

The articles here explore specific courses, design principles, and the occasional cross-domain comparison. Whether it is the front nine at Rustic Canyon, the mid-round brilliance of Ballybunion, or the parallels between baseball stadiums and golf holes, the through-line is the same: what makes a design work, and why?

Sam is based in the United States and can be found on X and Instagram.
